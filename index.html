Our Love Story

From a simple introduction in church to a lifetime of choosing each other.

01
The Introduction

It started in church.

Kachi was faithfully serving and growing in her walk at Dominion City when her mentor, who knew her heart and cared about her future, introduced her to Kanayo. What looked like a simple introduction would become the beginning of something beautiful.

02
Getting to Know Each Other

A friendship began.

There was no rush. They talked, laughed, shared stories and discovered how naturally they connected. Conversations became longer, meetings became more intentional, and friendship slowly made room for something deeper.

03
Growing Together

Faith, friendship & love.

As they grew closer, they found a relationship grounded in faith, honesty, prayer and genuine care. They began to see that love was not only about the beautiful moments, but also about choosing, supporting and understanding one another.

04
The Yes

A future worth choosing.

With time came certainty. Through prayers, conversations and countless little moments, Kachi and Kanayo became sure that they wanted to build their future together. Their story became less about coincidence and more about purpose.

05
Forever Begins

And now, they say “I do.”

Today, surrounded by family, friends and the people who have prayed for them, they celebrate the beginning of their next chapter. From one introduction in church to a promise of forever — this is Kachi & Kanayo.
